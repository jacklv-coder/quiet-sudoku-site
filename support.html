Quiet Sudoku · Support

A little room to think. Classic Sudoku for iPhone and iPad.

Continue a puzzle

Open Play, then Saved games. Progress saves automatically on your device. Enabling iCloud in Settings makes recent checkpoints available to your other Apple devices. Opening a remote checkpoint creates a separate local copy.

Use notes and hints

Select a cell, enable Notes, then choose digits. Guide first shows where to look, then explains the reasoning. You decide whether to apply the step. Undo and redo are available.

Make the board comfortable

Choose Paper or Chalkboard in Settings. Use Large number keys or Focus view for bigger controls. Rotate iPad to place the tools beside the board.

Daily puzzles and reminders

On Today, choose Browse past days to revisit the past year. Reminders are optional; completing today's puzzle cancels its upcoming reminder. Open the app periodically to refresh the next 60 days of reminders.

Restore access to iCloud

Sign into the same Apple account on both devices and enable iCloud checkpoints inside Quiet Sudoku. Choose Sync now, then refresh Saved games. Your local progress remains available while offline.
